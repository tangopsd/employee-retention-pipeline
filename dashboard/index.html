01

Data exploration

Before modeling, the data tells its own story: overworked mid-tenure employees with low satisfaction are the clearest churn signal in the dataset.

Class imbalance: ~1 in 6 employees left: the minority class the model needs to catch.
Overwork cluster: a dense group of leavers sits at 240+ hrs/month with satisfaction near zero.
Tenure risk window: attrition concentrates at 3–5 years, not at the extremes.
Project overload: employees on 6-7 projects work the longest hours and leave most.
Pay stagnation: long-tenured staff are disproportionately stuck on low/medium salary.
Not department-driven: attrition share is fairly even across departments, telling us this isn't a team-specific problem.
Correlation check: satisfaction level correlates negatively with attrition; number of projects correlates with monthly hours, as expected.
